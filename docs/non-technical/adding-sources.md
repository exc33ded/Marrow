# Adding sources

Paste a link into the box on Home, or into the search box (`Ctrl K`), or drop a PDF anywhere on the page.

| You paste | Marrow adds |
|---|---|
| A PDF file, or a link to one | a **paper** |
| An arXiv ID or arXiv link | the paper's PDF from arXiv |
| A DOI | whatever the DOI points to: the PDF if there is one, otherwise the article page |
| Any web page | an **article**, cleaned into a reader view without ads or menus |
| A YouTube link | a **video**, played next to its transcript |

If the link is already in your library, Marrow opens the copy you have instead of adding it again.

After a source is added, the AI writes its Brief automatically, as long as there is enough readable text. A scanned PDF with no selectable text can be read, but the AI features need text.

## Videos and transcripts

A video needs a transcript before the AI can work with it. There are three ways to get one:

- **YouTube's own captions**, fetched with one click once the *YouTube helper* is downloaded in Settings.
- **Whisper**, which transcribes the sound on your own computer. Set it up once in Settings. You can also transcribe any audio or video file you have.
- **Paste a transcript**: YouTube's "Show transcript" panel, an SRT or VTT file, or plain text.

Every note, card and AI answer on a video links to the moment in the video it refers to.

## Discover

- **Search papers.** Search 250 million scholarly works (OpenAlex) and add the open-access ones with one click.
- **Follow feeds.** Follow blogs, journals and arXiv categories by their address. Marrow finds the RSS or Atom feed from a blog's home page. New posts are listed with read/unread and a "read later" list, and each feed can be refreshed on its own. A feed with no posts yet can still be followed.
