# What Marrow is

Marrow is a research desk for one person. Papers, web articles and YouTube lectures go into one library. Instead of handing you a summary, Marrow helps you understand and remember each one.

## Who it is for

A researcher working on one or more projects, in long sessions at a desk, who needs to be able to explain a source in their own words and still remember it weeks later.

## The four steps

Every source, whatever its kind, goes through the same four steps:

1. **Brief.** The skeleton first: the problem, the core idea, the method, the results, the weaknesses, and what you need to know beforehand.
2. **Read / Watch.** Section by section. Each section ends with a checkpoint question that you answer from memory. Your answer is graded against the source.
3. **Teach.** Explain the source in your own words. The AI points out gaps and misunderstandings.
4. **Keep.** Flashcards that come back just before you would forget them, plus a concept map.

A source's progress ring blends these: checkpoints count 40%, the teach-back 35% and cards you have retained 25%.

## Principles

- **Understanding over summary.** You do the work, and the AI checks it.
- **Every claim links back.** AI-written cards and feedback must quote the source. Each quote is checked against the source's text, and anything that can't be found is flagged.
- **One place.** Everything you make (notes, cards, pages, maps) lives with the sources.
- **Comfort over long sessions.** Light and dark modes in every look, readable contrast, visible keyboard focus.

## Your data

Marrow runs on your own computer as a single-user app. There is no sign-in and no account in the cloud: your sources, notes and PDFs live in one folder on that machine.
